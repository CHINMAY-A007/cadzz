Who this is for

Cadzz is for businesses whose days are full of repetitive, rules-based work: enquiries, messages, documents, approvals and reports that follow the same steps every time. If the work runs on clear rules and hand-offs, it can usually be handled automatically, end to end, with a person only stepping in when it matters.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Teams with repetitive, high-volume work: sales, support, operations, logistics and back office. If a process runs on rules and messages, it can usually be automated.

Do you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We connect to your existing email, WhatsApp, CRM, spreadsheets and internal systems wherever they expose an API, so you keep working the way you do today.

How long does a project take?

Most first versions are live within a few weeks. Scope decides the timeline, so we agree it with you before any building starts.

Who owns the system and the data?

You do. We build on your accounts and hand over the systems, so nothing is locked inside us. No black boxes.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope. We scope it with you first so you know the shape and the cost before we build anything.
