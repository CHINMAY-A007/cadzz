Privacy Policy

Draft. This is a plain-language draft that describes what this site currently does. It must be reviewed by a qualified person before launch. It is not legal advice and it deliberately makes no legal claims.

Last updated: 2026.

What this page covers

This page explains what happens to the information you send through the contact form on the Cadzz website.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, the site collects the details you type in:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your company name, if you choose to provide it
  • Your email address
  • A short brief describing what you need

Why we collect it

We use these details to read your enquiry and reply to you about your requirements. Nothing else.

How the form sends your details

If a form endpoint is configured, your details are sent to that endpoint so we can receive them. If no endpoint is configured, the form opens your own email app with the message ready for you to send to us, and nothing leaves your device until you press send in that app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your details.
  • We do not use your details for advertising.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list.

Storage on your device

This site stores one small preference in your browser: your chosen colour theme. It is kept in localStorage so the site remembers it. There are no tracking cookies and no analytics on this site.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Fontshare and Google Fonts. Those providers receive the usual request information needed to serve the font files. If a form endpoint is configured, the provider that operates it processes the details you submit.

Keeping your details

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to follow up on your request.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them. Send your request to the email address below.
